# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (licensed CC BY 4.0). This is a short version written for U1 Doctor.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their age, experience, background, identity, language or country, in a way that is open, welcoming and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to 3D printing, to Klipper or to this tool.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Accept honest corrections. Say when you do not know.
- Keep reports truthful. Do not share someone else's private data (printer addresses, serial numbers, Wi-Fi names, files) without their clear yes.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination or threats.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or using the issue tracker for advertising.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, pull requests and any other space of this repository, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem privately, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/SirStormz/u1-doctor/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report. Please do not open a public issue about it.

The maintainer will read every report, keep it confidential, and may remove comments, close or lock a discussion, or block a user from the repository when behaviour breaks this code. This project is unofficial and not affiliated with Snapmaker.
