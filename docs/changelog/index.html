History

Every release, newest first. The full technical detail lives in the release notes on GitHub.

  1. 0.4.0-g111 Nov 2026

    The support pack

    An AES-256 zip you can hand to Snapmaker support, after the app has checked what goes in it. It also reads the recent jobs and the console errors of the printer.

  2. 0.3.0-g110 Nov 2026

    New screens

    Simple and Expert home screens, a guided Verdict whose camera follows each step, and a Diagnose screen with four modes. Each theme has its own pieces: meters, ticket, radar, liquid lens.

  3. 0.2.0-g110 Nov 2026

    Four themes

    Pupitre, Notice, Obsidienne and Visière replace the three old themes. A theme changes the look only, never what the app says.

  4. 0.1.9-g19 Nov 2026

    Read exported logs

    Import Moonraker, Klipper and Orca log files and run the new rules S25 to S32. VirusTotal: 0 detections out of 66 engines.

  5. 0.1.8-g18 Nov 2026

    Live rules and G-code checks

    Live rules S17 to S24 with a connection self-check, 10 community-sourced G-code checks, 3MF input, known issues per firmware, good-to-know notes and a sample report.

  6. 0.1.7-g18 Nov 2026

    Simple and Expert

    Two display levels and a first-run guide, so the app is readable on day one and deep when you need it.

  7. 0.1.6-g17 Nov 2026

    Starts empty

    The app opens with nothing in it. Demo mode is an explicit switch, and synthetic data is always badged.

  8. 0.1.0-g17 Nov 2026

    First release

    Offline decoder for the 413 official error codes, a deterministic rules engine, an observed timeline and the U1-DEB evidence bundle.
